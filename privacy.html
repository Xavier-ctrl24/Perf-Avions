Privacy Policy

Application: Takeoff & Landing Perf (package app.perfavions.runwayperf). Last updated: 3 August 2026.

The short version

This application collects nothing, stores nothing on any server, and has no user accounts. Everything you type stays on your device. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK of any kind.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. We have no server that could receive it, and no way to identify you.

Data stored on your device

So that you do not have to retype everything before each flight, the application saves the values you enter in your device's local browser storage (localStorage). This includes:

  • the aircraft performance figures you enter, and the optional name you give them;
  • the runway data you enter, including any latitude and longitude you choose to type in;
  • the weather values and safety margin you enter;
  • your unit preference (metric or imperial) and the fact that you accepted the disclaimer.

This information never leaves your device. It is not readable by us or by anyone else. Uninstalling the application, or clearing the app's storage from your device settings, deletes it permanently.

Network access

The application makes exactly one kind of network request, and only when you press the “Fetch METAR” button:

  • it sends the four-letter ICAO aerodrome code you typed to metar.vatsim.net, and if that service is unavailable to aviationweather.gov (NOAA Aviation Weather Center), in order to retrieve the public weather report for that aerodrome.

No personal data is included in that request: it contains the aerodrome code and nothing else. As with any internet request, the operators of those services receive your device's IP address and apply their own privacy policies, which are outside our control. If you never press that button, the application makes no network request at all and works fully offline.

Location

The application does not request or use your device's location. The optional latitude and longitude fields are typed in by you, taken from your aerodrome chart, and are used only for an on-device sunrise and sunset calculation. The app requests no location permission from Android.

Permissions

The Android application requests a single permission, INTERNET, used solely for the METAR request described above. It requests no access to storage, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Children

This is a flight-preparation tool for pilots. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date at the top.
