Dans le Play Console : Version → « Notes de version », un bloc par langue,
balisé `<en-US>` / `<fr-FR>`. Limite : 500 caractères par langue.

```
Second closed-test build.

- New "Rate this app" button next to the coffee link, so testers can leave feedback without hunting for the store listing.
- Housekeeping: unused prototype pages removed from the package.

Nothing changed in the performance model: the figures still come from the numbers you enter from your own flight manual, corrected for pressure altitude, temperature, wind and runway slope.
```
